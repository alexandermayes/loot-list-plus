<objective>
Turn the sprint plan's ten recommended candidate metrics into a committed, auditable menu: a query file for every metric the data can answer honestly, and a stated reason for every metric it cannot.

**As the** person who has to defend this report to a skeptic, **I want to** see every candidate metric with its real number or the exact reason it is missing, **so that** the findings I publish are chosen from the whole truth rather than from whatever happened to compute cleanly.

Purpose: the pipeline is proven by plan 03-01, so this plan is pure horizontal expansion along the one dimension that decides the report's quality: metric definitions. Three of the ten candidates are traps that would produce a plausible, publishable, wrong number if computed naively (the two funnel-timing metrics and the attendance-weighting presence check), and one was written off by research on a schema assumption that turns out to be false. Getting these right before the human picks findings is the whole job.

Output: a committed registry of ten metrics, seven committed query files, and a reproducible candidate menu.
</objective>
