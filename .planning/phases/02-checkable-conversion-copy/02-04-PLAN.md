<objective>
Make every proof claim on the homepage checkable: each testimonial says who said it, when, and how a reader can verify it, and the stat row states facts instead of an unmeasured time saving.

**As an** officer weighing whether to trust this product, **I want to** see who each quote came from and how I can check it myself, **so that** I can tell the difference between real customers and marketing copy.

Purpose: PROOF-01 and PROOF-02 are the reason this phase exists. Today the homepage attributes four quotes to a name and a guild with nothing a reader can check, and asserts a weekly time saving nothing measures. This plan replaces both with things a visitor can confirm.

Output: a testimonial block with per-quote verification and a stat row with only checkable facts, both pinned by an automated test.
</objective>
