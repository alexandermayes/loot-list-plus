<objective>
External consumers stay item-only (D-14): the companion and addon payloads (guild-data, export-string), the Gargul DFT block and the bot's `/priority` never see a need row, and the readers that can still meet one through a ranking or a BLP row skip it explicitly and warn once per request (D-16).

Purpose: research found the companion keys its item table by `String(wowhead_id)`, so a single need row would collide every need on "null" and corrupt the addon's data; the bot would answer "chest" with a need. These are the highest-blast-radius readers outside the web app.
Output: kind filters and need branches in five files, with route and unit tests.

D-22 note: no raider can rank a need before Phase 4 (the submit route and the save_submission_items hold refuse it), so the need-ranked lists in this plan's tests are fake-client fixtures that stand past the hold; they prove the export half of the lifecycle D-22 asks to be proven with fixtures.
