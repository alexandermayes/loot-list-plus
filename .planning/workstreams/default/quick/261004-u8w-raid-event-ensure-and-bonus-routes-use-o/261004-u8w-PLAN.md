New raid events made by POST /api/raid-events/bonus and POST /api/raid-events/ensure get their raid tier only from the request guild's own expansions, and bonus events get only that guild's raid teams. This is follow-up FU-1 of quick task 261003-she (.planning/workstreams/default/quick/261003-she-bulk-attendance-writes-stay-inside-the-o/261003-she-SUMMARY.md, finding 8 and FU-1).
