<objective>
GH #307 asks that an addon or companion award for an item listed in more than one raid tier of a guild be filed under the tier of the boss it dropped from, with tests for the AQ enchanting formulas and a TBC Tier 6 recipe. That suggested fix already shipped: PR #322 (2a727361, merged 2026-09-29, quick 260929-l3a) added the boss, boss-tier, raid and deterministic fallback rules to resolveGuildLootItem, made import strings use their own session's live boss kill and instance name, and added the tests (seven AQ formulas with C'Thun and Ossirian, recipe 32736 with Archimonde and Illidan Stormrage, fallback and single-tier cases, route tests for loot-award and import-string). PR #322 said "Refs #307" on purpose (user decision, its OD-4): companion awards still carry the cached catalog label, and #319 ("Completes #307") tracks sending the live boss and instance.

This plan closes the server-side gaps measured at 059357e1 against the real catalogs and the real addon:
1. The raid rule compares the session's instance name with the catalog tier name only. The game reports map names, and several differ from the catalog: "Ahn'Qiraj Temple" (Temple of Ahn'Qiraj), "Tempest Keep" (Tempest Keep: The Eye), and in the retail map table "The Battle for Mount Hyjal", "Coilfang: Serpentshrine Cavern", "The Sunwell". On Classic Era the addon names AQ40 kills from its own NPC table ("Vem", "Lord Kri", "Princess Yauj", "Emperor Vek'lor", "Emperor Vek'nilash"), which are not catalog boss names ("Silithid Royalty", "Twin Emperors"), so after those kills neither the boss nor the raid rule decides and the import keeps the addon's cached tier.
2. Nether Vortex (30183, Serpentshrine Cavern and Tempest Keep) and Heart of Darkness (32428, Hyjal Summit and Black Temple) sit under 'Crafting Materials' in both raids. That label is not a group label, so a live boss never decides them: 24 of 136 TBC live-boss cases fall back (13 or 11 to the wrong raid depending on tier id order).
