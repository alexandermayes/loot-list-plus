Enumerate every card rendered directly inside another card with a real ancestry check, present the list and a before/after at a blocking checkpoint, then convert the confirmed sites to the `nested` variant.

Output: the ancestry script, a confirmed conversion list, and the converted sites rendering `variant="nested"`.
</objective>

      - Leave it to the call site, so the primitive stays a single unconditional divider and a first-child instance passes a class to turn it off when it actually looks wrong.
