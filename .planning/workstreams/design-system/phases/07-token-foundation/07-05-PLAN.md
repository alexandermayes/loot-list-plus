Hue 38 is deliberate: it sits between `--accent` at hue 30 and `--warning` at hue 45, so standby reads as its own amber rather than as either of its neighbours (D-07). Light mode gets its own darker value for the same reason `--accent` and `--destructive` already do.

Contrast, measured during planning. The comparison column is the literal being replaced, Tailwind `orange-500` at `#f97316`:

| pair | orange-500 | --standby | floor |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| dark, as text on the lifted card | 5.884 | 7.716 | 4.5 |
| dark, as text on the page | 7.195 | 9.434 | 4.5 |
| light, as text on a white card | 2.803 | 3.003 | non-regression |
| light, as text on the cream page | 2.640 | 2.828 | non-regression |
| `--standby-foreground` on `--standby`, dark | n/a | 9.251 | 4.5 |
| `--standby-foreground` on `--standby`, light | n/a | 6.585 | 4.5 |

D-07 requires standby to "pass contrast on both themes". That is operationalised here as two testable claims rather than one vague one, because a flat 4.5:1 text floor is not the standard the rest of this palette meets in light mode: light `--warning` as text on white measures 1.983, light `--success` 2.298 and light `--accent` 3.093. Holding a new token to a floor none of its siblings meets would be inventing a requirement rather than satisfying one. So the two claims are: a real 4.5:1 WCAG floor everywhere standby is a fill with text on it, and everywhere standby is itself text, a strict improvement on the literal it replaces, on every surface, in both themes. Light-mode status-token text contrast as a whole is a real finding and is recorded as such for Phase 12 and milestone B; it is not this phase's requirement and is not silently absorbed either.

The two substitutions differ in kind and the commit bodies must say so. Replacing `yellow-500` with `--warning` is provably a rename: `#eab308` versus `#e7b008`, a difference of 3 in red and 3 in green and none in blue. Replacing `orange-500` with `--standby` is a deliberate recolour: `#f97316` versus `#f59f0a`, hue 25 to hue 38, which is the point of D-07 and is exactly what the screenshot review is for.
</value_table>
