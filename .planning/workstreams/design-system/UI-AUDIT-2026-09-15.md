## Root causes
1. Type scale floor is 10px (tailwind.config.js xs=10, sm=12, base=13); 11px and 15px missing from the scale; 1,044 arbitrary text-[Npx] classes (255 x 13px, 253 x 12px, 150 x 11px, 96 x 10px, 13 x 9px); components/ui/label.tsx spells its own sizes as text-[12px]/[13px]/[14px].
2. Dark --foreground-muted (#666666, L40%) measures 3.35:1 on base and 3.18:1 on elevated; 143 direct uses. Light --accent (30 100% 45%) is 3.1:1 on white across 305 text-accent uses. Fix candidates: muted L52% (#858585) = 5.2:1 base, 4.9:1 elevated.
3. Surface ramp invisible on dark: --border 1.07:1 against elevated; elevated vs inset 1.045:1. --background-inset is commented "Nested cards inside elevated" (globals.css:136,213), used 12 times.
4. Two brands: marketing purple #9940ec (25 uses) plus off-token #bababa (59) and #080808 (19); app accent #ff8000. Hero shimmer (.text-shimmer-purple, globals.css:51-75) is gradient text on hover and LandingCompare.tsx:85 applies it to competitors' names.
5. Dead tokens, live literals: --alliance/--horde defined, used 0 times (GuildSettingsContent.tsx:565,585 hard-codes blue-500/red-500); 432 hex literals; 129 Tailwind palette classes (48 in LootListContent.tsx, 16 in status-badge.tsx, alert.tsx warning variant).
6. Five label conventions (9/10/11/12/13px, three tracking values) and two kicker primitives: LabelText (typography.tsx:135-148) and .section-label (globals.css:570-573).
7. Card primitive used in 10 files; its class string hand-copied 166 times.
