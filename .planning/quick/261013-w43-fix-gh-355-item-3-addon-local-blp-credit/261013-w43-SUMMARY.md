# Quick 261013-w43: Addon local BLP credits a raider once per item per raid night (GH #355 item 3)

The addon's local bad luck protection counter incremented every listed non-winner on every award, so two copies of one item on one night credited everyone twice. The website credits each raider at most once per item per raid night and gives the winner no credit at the event where they won. The addon now does the same.

## Commits (addon repo, branch fix/blp-once-per-night, PR alexandermayes/loot-list-plus-addon#2)

- `f852032d` fix: credit bad luck protection once per raider per item per raid night

## What changed

- `Core/DB.lua`: profile-level `blpCredited[raidDate][lootItemId][characterId]` marker (survives guild-data imports, which replace `blp`), with `DB:WasBLPCredited` and `DB:MarkBLPCredited`; marking a new night drops older nights, and 1.1.0 saved files without the key are guarded.
- `Modules/LootDistribution.lua`: `LD:CurrentRaidDate()` returns the Attendance session's `raidDate` when tracking, else today (UTC, as the addon stamps everything). `LD:AwardItem` resets the winner as before, marks the winner as handled for the night, and increments each other listed raider only when not yet credited for this item tonight.
- `tests/test_blp.lua` (6 tests): two copies credit a non-winner once; the winner stays at 0 after a later copy; an import between the two awards does not lead to a second increment; a new raid date credits again; a different item credits independently.
- `CHANGELOG.md`: bullet under a new "Unreleased" heading. No version bump (releasing is the owner's call).
